A public space where notes grow, interlink, and are cultivated. Typically, the maturity of a note is shown through a borrowed nomenclature of seedlings through to evergreens. 
The idea (from what I can find) was developed by [[Maggie Appleton\|Maggie Appleton]] and you can see a walkthrough of the journey in her [History & Ethos](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history) page in her garden.

I see this as an opportunity not to have to produce a publication schedule or even coherently order the stuff I'm doing as the links rather than chronology does the work of building the garden. 

Found useful resources from [[Wanderloots\|Wanderloots]] (including how to set this up) and the discussions in the [[Decapsulate\|Decapsulate]] podcast. All obviously helped by the fact I have been using [[obsidian\|obsidian]] for years and love a productivity push.
