I am lucky to have been part of a movement at Oxford to try and better the research culture that has provided me with a number of unique experiences while trying to speak up for others. 

Most recently that takes the form of being a UK Research Staff Association representative on the Researcher Development Concordat Strategy Group led by Prof Richard Emes. 

In a meeting recently I was reminded of the a Taoist principle that the best leader helps in an invisible way such that when the work is done people say they did it themselves. Tao Te Ching (Dao De Jing) Chapter 17 by Lao Tzu.
